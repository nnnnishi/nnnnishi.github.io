Survey

Tables of papers, datasets, evaluation metrics, and libraries on topics I follow. The survey pages and the explainer pages are written in Japanese.

  • Updated {{ site.data.survey_budget_uplift.updated }} Budget-constrained uplift modeling (in Japanese)
    Papers, datasets, metrics, and libraries on uplift modeling that decides who receives coupons or incentives under budget and cost constraints. Key papers have unofficial explainer pages.
  • Updated {{ site.data.survey_optimal_tree.updated }} Optimal decision trees (in Japanese)
    Papers, datasets, metrics, and libraries on learning decision trees by optimization rather than greedy splitting: mixed-integer optimization, dynamic programming and branch-and-bound, SAT and constraint programming, continuous optimization, and prescriptive trees. Key papers have unofficial explainer pages.
  • Updated {{ site.data.survey_pricing.updated }} Data-driven pricing optimization (in Japanese)
    Papers, datasets, metrics, and libraries that combine demand estimation with price optimization: predict-then-optimize, dynamic pricing, personalized pricing, robust pricing, and price endogeneity.
  • Updated {{ site.data.survey_reciprocal_rec.updated }} Recommendation in two-sided markets (reciprocal recommendation and matching) (in Japanese)
    Papers, datasets, metrics, and libraries on recommending users to other users in two-sided markets such as job matching and online dating.
